Site Plan - Explore Brazil

Site Name

Explore Brazil (possible domain: explorebrazilguide.com)

I chose this name because it says exactly what the site is about: a guide to explore the best travel destinations in Brazil. It is short, easy to remember and works well for people searching for trip ideas.

Site Purpose

The site is a travel guide that presents the best destinations in Brazil. It provides a list of places with photos, the region and state where they are located, the type of attraction (beach, nature, city or historic), the best season to visit and a short description. Users can filter destinations by region, save favorites in the browser and send questions or suggestions through a contact form.

Scenarios

  • "I have vacation in July. Which places in Brazil are good to visit during that month?"
  • "I love nature and waterfalls. Which region of Brazil should I choose for my next trip?"

Color Schema

  • Dark Green (#1b4332) - headers, footer, headings and main buttons
  • Yellow (#ffd60a) - call to action buttons and highlights
  • Light Gray Green (#f4f7f4) - card and section backgrounds

These colors are inspired by the brazilian flag and are used on this document too.

Typography

  • Poppins (600) - used for all headings, like the ones in this document
  • Open Sans (400, 700) - used for paragraphs and general text, like this one
