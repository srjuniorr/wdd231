Why Brazil?

Brazil is the fifth largest country in the world and has a bit of everything: tropical beaches, the biggest rainforest on the planet, historic colonial towns and modern cities full of life.

When to Go

The country is huge, so the best season changes from place to place. Each destination in our guide shows the best months to visit so you can plan the right trip.

How this Guide Works

Browse the destinations page, filter by region, open the details and mark your favorites with a star. Your favorites list is saved in your browser for the next visit.
